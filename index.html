We make the feed ourselves

Most poultry in Pakistan is raised on commercial feed built for fast weight gain. Ours is ground on the farm from four things, and the birds get it once a day. The rest of what they eat, they find themselves.

Maize

The base of the mix

Wheat

Grown and ground locally

Chana

Protein without additives

Sarson

Mustard for condition and shine

No growth promoters and no commercial layer feed. If you want to see the mix before you order, ask us on WhatsApp and we will send a photo of that week's batch.

A day on the farm

The difference between our eggs and the ones in the shop is simply how the hens spend their time.

Out at first light

The hens are let out in the morning and stay on the fields all day, scratching for insects, seeds and greens.

One feed a day

They are fed our own maize, wheat, chana and sarson mix once daily. Everything else they eat, they forage.

Collected and packed daily

Eggs are gathered each day and packed as orders come in, so what reaches you is usually a day or two old at most.

Questions people ask

How are these different from shop eggs?

Most eggs sold in shops come from hens kept indoors on commercial feed. Ours come from hens that are outside all day finding much of their own food, which is why the yolks are darker and firmer.

Do you use growth promoters or antibiotics in the feed?

Our feed is ground on the farm from maize, wheat, chana and sarson. Nothing else is added to it.

What if some eggs break in transit?

Tell us on WhatsApp with a photo on the day you receive them and we will replace them in your next delivery.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver around add your city and the areas you cover. If you are outside that area, message us and we will tell you whether we can reach you.

How fresh are the eggs?

They are collected daily from the same flock and sent out as orders come in, so they are usually a day or two old at most.
